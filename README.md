**All the buttons. Not a bar.**

Loose Buttons puts your spells and window shortcuts wherever you want on the Forever HUD. No action bar. Open your spellbook, hit the Loose Buttons tab, and drag a spell or launcher onto open ground.

Drop next to another button and it snaps flush. Green edges show the link before you let go. Drop into a gap and it fills. Drop on the seam and the row slides open. Shift-drag moves the whole group. Alt-drag peels one free. Shift-right-click nukes a group. Alt-right-click nukes one button. Lock it when you're done so you don't fat-finger your layout mid-pull.

**Also in the mix**

- Icons tint like the default action bar. Blue means not enough power, gray means it cannot be used, and full color means it can. `/loose tint` turns that off.
- Themes and scale (including Character & info launchers)
- Layouts you can save, share (`LB1!...`), and swap per character
- Keybinds. Hit Edit, hover a button, then press a key, a mouse button, the wheel, or a controller button. Blizzard's Quick Keybind does the same thing.
- Macros and consumables land like spells. Launchers open your usual panels. Blizzard blocks those clicks in combat.

`/loose` opens Edit. `/loose help` is the cheat sheet. `/loose reset` wipes every button.

Built for **WoW Forever** (not Classic-era). Free, full source.
